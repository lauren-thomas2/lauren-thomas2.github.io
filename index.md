### About
I'm a graduate student working in the [RIVER Lab](https://www.colorado.edu/lab/liningerfluvialgeomorphgroup/) in the Department of Geography at CU Boulder. My current research combines field work and geospatial analyses to answer questions about river and floodplain carbon storage and transport. For my master's thesis, I've been studying how human alteration of floodplains has affected the amount of carbon stored in soil, vegetation, and downed wood in floodplains in the Midwest and Southwest United States. For my PhD, my research focuses on improving understanding of hydrologic connectivity and fluxes in deltas on the North Slope of Alaska.
